## Short post

I built Pane, an open-source Sine mod for Zen Browser. It lets you replace one tab inside a split view without unsplitting or losing the pane positions and divider sizes.

It has a keyboard-first glass picker, recent-tab shortcuts, search, appearance presets, and safety checks around Zen's split layout. It is early, working, and ready for testers and contributors.

Try it, open an issue, or send a PR: https://github.com/001vamp/zen-pane-manager

## Community post

### Pane: swap a tab inside a Zen split without rebuilding the layout

I kept running into a small but frustrating Zen workflow: I had a split arranged exactly how I wanted, but changing one pane meant unsplitting, rebuilding, and resizing everything.

Pane fixes that. Focus a pane, press `Ctrl+Alt+R` or its ⇄ button, and choose another open tab. The new tab takes the same leaf in Zen's split tree, so the layout and divider ratios stay intact.

The project is open source under MPL-2.0. The first release includes search, recently used tabs, keyboard navigation, glass presets, configurable appearance, rollback behavior, and no telemetry or network requests.

I would especially appreciate compatibility testing, accessibility feedback, bug reports with reproducible steps, and focused PRs. The roadmap is public and newcomer-sized contributions are welcome.

Repository and installation: https://github.com/001vamp/zen-pane-manager
